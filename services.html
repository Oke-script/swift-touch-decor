Working Sectors

We are an Interior Design Company Who believe in excellence, quality and honesty. Yes, we design beautiful home interiors.

Concept Designs

At Swift Touch Decor, our concept design stage is where creativity meets purpose. We develop unique design ideas that reflect your vision, lifestyle, and space requirements. From mood boards to color palettes and layout planning, we craft a clear design direction that sets the foundation for a beautiful and functional interior.

Project Designs

Our Project Design service covers every detail from concept to completion. We create customized interior plans that balance beauty, comfort, and functionality — ensuring each project reflects your style while meeting practical needs. Every design is carefully curated to bring your dream space to life.

Make Overs

Whether it’s refreshing a single room or transforming your entire space, our Make Over service gives your environment a new life. We reimagine layouts, colors, and décor elements to create a fresh, modern, and inspiring look — without losing the essence of what makes your space unique.

Consulting

Our Consulting service provides expert guidance for clients who need professional input before making design decisions. From color choices to material selection and layout advice, we help you make informed choices that enhance your space and fit your budget.

Furniture & Fixtures

We offer Fittings and Furniture solutions that combine style, durability, and comfort. Our team helps you select or design furniture and fittings that perfectly match your space, ensuring a seamless blend of aesthetics and functionality in every corner.

Space Planning

Effective Space Planning is at the heart of every great design. We analyze your space and lifestyle to create layouts that maximize flow, comfort, and usability. Every inch is thoughtfully organized to ensure balance, purpose, and beauty in your environment.

Some Special Services

Lighting

At Swift Touch Decor, we understand that lighting sets the mood of every space. Our Lighting design focuses on creating the perfect balance between natural and artificial light to enhance ambiance, highlight key features, and bring warmth and depth to your interiors.

02

Coloring

Coloring is the soul of design — it defines mood, personality, and emotion. We carefully select color palettes that reflect your style and create harmony throughout your space, whether you prefer bold contrasts or calm, neutral tones.

03

Patterns

Our Pattern designs add character and rhythm to every interior. From subtle geometric shapes to rich, artistic motifs, we use patterns thoughtfully to create balance, movement, and visual interest without overwhelming your space.

04

Textures

Textures bring life and dimension to any room. By combining materials like wood, fabric, stone, and metal, we create a rich sensory experience that feels both inviting and sophisticated — making every surface tell a story of elegance and comfort.
