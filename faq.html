When do I need an Interior Designer?

You need an interior designer when you’re planning to renovate, redecorate, or set up a new space and want it to reflect your personal style while maintaining functionality. Whether it’s a new home, office, or commercial space, hiring a designer early in the process helps you save time, avoid costly mistakes, and bring your vision to life seamlessly.

Why do I need an Interior Designer?

An interior designer helps you transform your ideas into a beautiful, practical space. We understand design principles, materials, lighting, and color coordination to ensure every detail fits perfectly. With a professional designer, you get expert guidance, access to trusted suppliers, and a result that feels cohesive, stylish, and tailored to your taste and budget.

Why do you have a design fee and purchasing fee on the product?

The design fee covers the creative process — space planning, concept development, mood boards, and drawings that bring your vision to life. The purchasing fee covers sourcing, ordering, and coordinating deliveries of furniture, décor, and materials. This ensures quality control and saves you the stress of dealing with multiple vendors.

What should I have at our first meeting for my project?

Bring along any inspiration materials — photos, Pinterest boards, floor plans, or color preferences. If it’s an existing space, having measurements or pictures of the current layout is also helpful. Most importantly, come with an open mind and a clear idea of your goals, style, and budget range.

How much will my interior design project cost?

The cost of your project depends on its size, scope, and the materials or furnishings selected. After an initial consultation, we’ll provide a tailored estimate that fits your budget while maintaining quality and design excellence. We’ll discuss your budget range upfront so you’re fully informed and comfortable with the financial commitment.

How long will it take to to design and build my project?

Timelines vary depending on the complexity of the project and product availability. On average, design concepts can take 1–3 weeks, while implementation and setup can take several additional weeks. We’ll provide a detailed schedule after reviewing your project requirements.
